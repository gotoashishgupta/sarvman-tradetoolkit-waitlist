What we do with the details you type in

What we collect

The waitlist form collects your email address, your company name, which kind of work you do most, and which calculators you size most often. The consent box is recorded as yes or no so we can show what was agreed.

Why we collect it

We email you once, when the calculators go live. We also count the signups and where they came from, so we know whether to keep building this for the kind of contractor who signs up. That count is anonymous: it is a number, not a list.

Where it goes

The details are delivered by email to a company mailbox that Sarvman reads. Delivery goes through a third-party form relay, which forwards each submission to that mailbox; we use it for nothing else. The details are not sold, not shared with advertisers, and not passed to any third party for marketing.

The page itself sets no cookie and runs no third-party script. It keeps a random identifier in your own browser’s local storage, so that it can count visits without counting the same person twice on every page load. It holds no personal data, it is not sent anywhere except in the form submissions above, and clearing your browser data removes it.

How long we keep it

We keep the list until the calculators launch and for 30 days after, then delete it. If you ask us to remove you sooner, we delete it straight away.

Getting removed

Every email we send has an unsubscribe link, and you can also reply to any email from us and ask to be removed. We do not keep a suppression list beyond the 30-day window, so you can be added again later if you change your mind.

The honest limit

This page is a waitlist. It is not the calculator site, and nothing on it is engineering, legal, building-code or HVAC advice.
