What we do with the details you type in

What we collect

The waitlist form collects your email address, your company name, which kind of work you do most, and which calculators you size most often. The consent box is recorded as yes or no so we can show what was agreed.

There is also an optional question box. If you write a question there, we collect that text as well as the email address you gave, your company name, and the page you were on, and it is kept separately from the waitlist list so that it cannot be counted as a signup. You can leave the box empty and nothing extra is collected. A question needs an email address, so a question can only be sent if you also tick the box that lets us email you about the launch.

Why we collect it

We email you once, when the calculators go live. We also count the signups and where they came from, so we know whether to keep building this for the kind of contractor who signs up. That count is anonymous: it is a number, not a list.

If you write a question, it goes to our support desk so somebody can answer it. We answer questions from this box within 1 business day, by email, to the address you gave us. A business day is Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 US Eastern. The question is not used for marketing, and it is not part of the signup count. Treat the box as an email to a stranger: do not put anything in it you would not want us to read.

Where it goes

The details are sent to us over an encrypted connection and stored by us. We use them only to send the one email you asked for, and to count signups and where they came from. We do not sell them, do not share them with advertisers, and do not pass them to any marketing service.

A question you write is stored in the same way, in the same place, and is read by the support desk. It is not sent to any other service and it is not used to decide who gets the launch email. Our answer goes back to you by email, to the address you gave us, and we keep a copy of your question and our answer in the email account we use to answer questions. That account is held by our email provider, so they hold that copy on our behalf, and it is deleted on the same schedule as the question itself.

The page itself sets no cookie and runs no third-party script. It keeps a random identifier in your own browser’s local storage, so that it can count visits without counting the same person twice on every page load. It is not a name or an address, but we send it with a signup, so we can link a visit to the email address you gave us.

How long we keep it

We keep the list until the calculators launch and for 30 days after, then delete it. If you ask us to remove you sooner, we delete it straight away.

A question is kept until it has been answered and for 30 days after that, then deleted. The copy of the question and our answer in our email account is deleted on the same schedule. If you ask us to remove it sooner, we delete it straight away.

Getting removed

To ask us to delete your details, or to tell us to stop contacting you, use the question box on the waitlist page, or reply to any email we have sent you. We will respond within 30 days. If you use the question box, we will also take you off the launch list.

If you ask us to stop contacting you, we stop, and we do not add you back to the list.
