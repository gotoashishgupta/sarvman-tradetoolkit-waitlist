What we do with the details you type in

What we collect

The waitlist form collects your email address, your company name, which kind of work you do most, and which calculators you size most often. The consent box is recorded as yes or no so we can show what was agreed.

There is also an optional question box. If you write a question there, we collect that text as well as the email address you gave, and it is kept separately from the waitlist list so that it cannot be counted as a signup. You can leave the box empty and nothing extra is collected.

Why we collect it

We email you once, when the calculators go live. We also count the signups and where they came from, so we know whether to keep building this for the kind of contractor who signs up. That count is anonymous: it is a number, not a list.

If you write a question, it goes to our support desk so somebody can answer it. The question is not used for marketing, and it is not part of the signup count. Treat the box as an email to a stranger: do not put anything in it you would not want us to read.

Where it goes

The details are sent over an encrypted connection to a capture service we operate, and they are stored there. That connection is tunnelled through a third-party network, so that provider carries the request in transit. We use the details only to send the one email you asked for, and to count signups and where they came from. We do not sell them, do not share them with advertisers, and do not pass them to any marketing service.

A question you write is stored in the same way, in the same place, and is read by the support desk. It is not sent to any other service and it is not used to decide who gets the launch email.

The page itself sets no cookie and runs no third-party script. It keeps a random identifier in your own browser’s local storage, so that it can count visits without counting the same person twice on every page load. It holds no personal data, it is not sent anywhere except in the form submissions above, and clearing your browser data removes it.

How long we keep it

We keep the list until the calculators launch and for 30 days after, then delete it. If you ask us to remove you sooner, we delete it straight away.

A question is kept until it has been answered and for 30 days after that, then deleted. If you ask us to remove it sooner, we delete it straight away.

Getting removed

Every email we send has an unsubscribe link, and you can also reply to any email from us and ask to be removed. We do not keep a suppression list beyond the 30-day window, so you can be added again later if you change your mind.
