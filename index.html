Built for the contractor who has no desk software

You’re not losing money on the math. You’re losing the hour.

Most small HVAC crews do the sizing in a spreadsheet, a PDF printout from a supplier, or a group chat message. Each of those takes twenty minutes, can’t be re-run on site, and doesn’t end up attached to the job file.

These calculators are one page, they work with no signal, and the result is a sheet you can hand to the installer.
