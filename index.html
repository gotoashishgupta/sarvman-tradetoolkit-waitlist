What’s coming

The calculators we’re starting with, in the order crews ask for them. The final list depends on what crews tell us they size most often.

Load calculation

Room-by-room heat gain & loss, sensible & latent, with a printable load schedule per space.

Duct sizing

Supply, return and branch runs, round and rectangular, with friction and pressure drop.

Refrigerant charge

Superheat, subcooling and line-set sizing for the equipment on the truck.

Filter sizing

Face area, velocity, pressure drop and static resistance for any filter media.

Heat pump sizing

Capacity at design temperature, backup heat, and matched equipment in one sheet.

Spec sheets

Printable equipment and material specs — unit schedules, takeoff lists, and job-file sheets.

Built for the contractor who has no desk software

Where the sizing happens today

Most small HVAC crews do the sizing in a spreadsheet, a supplier PDF printout, or a group chat message. Each of those is hard to re-run on site, and it doesn’t end up attached to the job file.

The goal: one page per calculation you can re-run on site, with a result sheet you can hand to the installer.

What it costs

Nothing. The calculators are free to use, and there is no account to create.
