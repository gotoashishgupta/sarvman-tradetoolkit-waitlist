What we are building

The kinds of calculators in the first release. The final list is set before launch.

Load calculation

Heat gain and heat loss estimates per room, sensible and latent, with a printable load schedule.

Duct sizing

Supply, return and branch runs, round and rectangular, with friction and pressure drop.

Refrigerant charge

Superheat, subcooling and line-set sizing for the equipment on the truck.

Filter sizing

Face area, velocity, pressure drop and static resistance for common filter media.

Heat pump sizing

Capacity at design temperature, backup heat, and matched equipment in one sheet.

Unit conversions

BTU/h to tons, CFM to cubic feet per minute, refrigerant units and the rest of the conversions you look up on the roof.

Spec sheets

Printable equipment and material specs — unit schedules, takeoff lists and job sheets.

Built for the contractor who has no desk software

You’re not losing money on the math. You’re losing the hour.

When the sizing happens in a spreadsheet, a supplier’s PDF printout or a message in a group chat, it takes time, it can’t be re-run on site, and it doesn’t end up attached to the job file.

Each calculator will be a single page. It will work with no signal, show the working, and print as a sheet you can hand to the installer.
